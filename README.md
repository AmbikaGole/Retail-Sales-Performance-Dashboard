# Retail Sales Performance Dashboard

## Overview
This project analyses retail transaction data across multiple regions, 
customer segments and product categories to identify commercial performance 
gaps and support data-driven management decisions.

## Dataset
Retail transaction dataset — 100,000 rows | $138M total sales
Raw dataset not included in this repository.

## Objectives
Build an interactive Power BI dashboard to compare regional, customer and 
product performance, identify underperforming areas and provide prioritised 
recommendations to improve commercial outcomes.

## Methodology
- Data cleaning and transformation using Power Query
- DAX-calculated date table for time intelligence analysis
- Customer segmentation across five types: Wholesale, Corporate, 
  Retail, Online and VIP
- Regional and product category performance benchmarking
- Root-cause analysis of performance gaps

## Key Results
- Total sales analysed: $138M across the full dataset
- Identified a $27M revenue gap between the South and West regions
- South Region generated 4x the volume of the West Region ($36M vs $9M)

## Key Insights
- The West Region is significantly underperforming relative to its size 
  and potential — root-cause hypotheses include product mix misalignment, 
  local competition and supply chain delays
- Miscellaneous products dominate sales ($81M) while Furniture contributes 
  the least ($3M) — indicating opportunities for category optimisation
- Wholesale and Corporate customer types drive the highest revenue, 
  suggesting B2B channels should be prioritised for growth

## Tools
